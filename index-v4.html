About the Ogoni Skills and Career Development Database

Understanding our mission, vision, and the purpose behind building a comprehensive skills and career development platform.

What We Do

What is the Ogoni Skills and Career Development Database?

The Ogoni Skills and Career Development Database is a purpose-built platform created to identify, register, profile, develop, empower, support, and connect Ogoni people with skills development opportunities, career advancement programmes, sponsorship opportunities, empowerment initiatives, and strategic support systems.

It addresses the critical gap in structured data about Ogoni human capital, skills distribution, and career development needs.

The platform serves as the central hub for all skills and career development activities, bringing together beneficiaries, benefactors, diaspora supporters, programme partners, and administrators.

The master plan outlines a phased approach to building human capital, creating sustainable career pathways, and empowering communities through strategic skills development and support systems.

Process

Your Journey on the Platform

A step-by-step guide through the entire process on the Ogoni Skills and Career Development Database.

1. Create an Account

Register with your basic information. Choose your role — beneficiary, benefactor, or diaspora supporter.

2. Complete Your Profile

Fill in personal information, educational background, work experience, skills, and career aspirations.

3. Upload Documents & Apply

Submit identification, certificates, and required documents. Browse and apply for programmes.

4. Review, Approval & Assignment

Applications are reviewed. Upon approval, get assigned to programmes, matched with sponsors or mentors.

5. Complete, Certify & Grow

Complete your programme, earn your certificate, and continue accessing career development opportunities.
